# Hey, I'm Ruman Sidhu👋

MSc Data Science student at the University of Bath with a background in Electronics & Communication Engineering. Before Bath, I completed the first year of the MS in Data Science at Seattle University. I build things at the intersection of code, healthcare, and curiosity, with a focus on making AI actually useful in clinical and oncology settings.

I've worked across the full data science workflow: cleaning messy datasets, building predictive models, writing SQL pipelines, and deploying ML experiments. Lately I'm most interested in healthcare applications, from tumor classification to clinical decision support, alongside big data systems (HDFS/Hadoop) and graph-based ML.

When I'm not writing Python or R, I'm usually writing fiction. Published author with a novella and a co-authored anthology, and a full-length novel on the way.
